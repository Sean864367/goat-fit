# GOAT Fit — Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your GOAT Fit data in either of these ways:

**In the app (immediate, on-device data):**
Profile → Delete All Data erases everything stored on the device.

**Account and cloud data:**
Email seansarrasin+goatfit@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my GOAT Fit account". Your account and all cloud-synced entries (meals, workouts, weight, sleep, dose logs) are permanently deleted within 30 days, and you will get a confirmation email when it is done.

Deleting your account does not cancel anything else — GOAT Fit has no subscriptions or payments.
