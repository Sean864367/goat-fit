# GOAT Fit — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

GOAT Fit is a personal health and fitness tracker (meals, workouts, weight, sleep, and personal dose logs). It does not provide medical advice.

**On-device by default.** Everything you enter is stored on your device. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics, and it never sells your data.

**Optional account and cloud sync.** If you choose to create an account and sign in, the entries you log are also stored in a private cloud space (hosted on Google Firebase) that only your account can access, so your devices — and the assistant features you ask for — stay in sync. You can use the app fully without an account; sync is off until you sign in.

**Assistant features.** If you use the in-app assistant (for example, logging a meal by photo or voice), what you send is processed only to create the log entries you asked for.

**Your controls.** You can erase all on-device data at any time from Profile → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app. To delete your account and its cloud data, see the account deletion page in this repository (delete-account.md) or contact us.
