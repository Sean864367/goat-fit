# Daily Fuel — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

Daily Fuel stores everything you enter — meals, workouts, weight, sleep, dose logs, and health records — only on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising, and it does not collect, sell, or share your personal or health information. If you choose Export, the app creates a data file that goes only where you send it. You can erase all app data at any time from Profile → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app. Daily Fuel is a personal tracking tool and does not provide medical advice.
